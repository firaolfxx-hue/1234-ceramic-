Quality Assurance & Operations

1234 Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

To guarantee high product standards, zero shipment breakage, and flawless tile alignment, 1234 Finishing strictly adheres to standardized operating procedures across our warehouses and logistics network.

SOP-01

Material Receiving & Quality Audits

Every batch of ceramic tiles arriving from manufacturing partners undergoes a multi-point quality check before entering our inventory.

  • Caliber & Sizing Audit: Tiles are sampled to verify standard length, width, and right-angle edge perfection (0.1% tolerance).
  • Shade Tone Matching: Color shades are categorized under unified batch numbers to prevent floor color variations.
  • Water Absorption Rating: Porcelain tiles are lab-tested to verify absorption rates below 0.5%.
SOP-02

Warehouse Climate Control & Palleting

Finishing chemicals like tile adhesives, grouts, and sealants require specific storage conditions to preserve bonding strength.

  • Moisture Protection: Polymer adhesives and grouts are stored on elevated wooden pallets in dry, temperature-controlled zones.
  • FIFO Inventory Cycling: First-In, First-Out rules ensure fresh chemical compounds are delivered to jobsites.
  • Heavy Paver Stacking: Heavy porcelain crates are stored using certified forklift safety protocols.
SOP-03

Order Dispatch & Freight Protection

Transportation is where micro-cracks can occur if materials aren't secured properly. We enforce strict loading protocols.

  • Corner Edge Guarding: Tile boxes are wrapped in protective shrink-film with reinforced corner protectors.
  • Crate Strapping: High-density pallets are strapped to transport vehicles to prevent shifting during transit.
  • Driver Safety Briefing: Transport drivers are trained in safe unloading procedures at active building sites.
SOP-04

After-Sales Technical Guidance

We support masons and installation teams with technical specs for proper trowel notch sizing and adhesive mixing ratios.

  • Substrate Preparation Advice: Guidelines on floor leveling before laying porcelain slabs.
  • Joint Gap Calibration: Recommending minimum tile joint widths (2mm to 4mm) for thermal expansion.
  • Customer Satisfaction Follow-up: Post-delivery inspection calls for commercial wholesale clients.
